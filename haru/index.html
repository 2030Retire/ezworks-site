What it does

Files itself

Every recording routes to the folder you chose for that category — work meetings to your work drive, personal notes to your personal one. No renaming, no dragging.

Transcribes and summarizes automatically

Summaries with key points and action items, plus the full transcript. Passages the engine wasn't sure about are flagged for a quick review.

Learns your vocabulary

Fix a term once — company names, project codes, jargon — and every future note gets it right.

Lives in your tools

Notes are plain Markdown files saved next to the audio: searchable in Windows Explorer, readable in Obsidian, and optionally mirrored to OneNote.

Never loses a recording

Recordings are preserved through crashes, dead batteries, and lost connections. Nothing is deleted until it is safely uploaded.

Your keys. Your storage. Your data.

EZHaru has no server. Audio goes from your phone to your own OneDrive, and transcription runs on an AI service you connect with your own API key — under your own agreement, at cost, with no middleman. We never see, store, or touch your recordings. Read the privacy policy.
